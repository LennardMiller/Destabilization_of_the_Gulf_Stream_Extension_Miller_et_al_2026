This repository includes all data necessary to reproduce the figures in the Article "Enhanced Gulf Stream Path Variability under Intensified Stratification" (Miller et al., 2026).

Due to size constrains the data used to create the figures is not the immediate simulation output. Instead, data at intermediate processing level is provided here. Most of this data are time-averages which are taken over all available data after the removal of 2 initial years of spin-up, resulting in at least 5 years of simulation time for all variables presented here. For the path lines in Figures 2, S5, S6 and the AMOC stream function in Figure S2, functions that were used for their calculation from raw simulation data are also added in the notebooks although they are not required to reproduce the figures.
